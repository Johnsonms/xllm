# H200 report (not started)

No H200 run yet. `H200_PLAN.md` (next to this file) is the plan written at the end of the H100 work: H200 is the
same sm_90a silicon with 141 GB HBM3e at 4.8 TB/s (1.43x), so (1) the memory-bound kernels (quantisation, SwiGLU,
elementwise, norms, TSDN, conv, AWM combine; 2.6-2.7 TB/s on H100) get up to ~1.4x faster and the step shifts
further towards the GEMMs, which makes the fp8 GEMMs and DeepGEMM worth relatively more; (2) the 141 GB remove the
memory trade-offs made on H100: `recompute_fc1_out / fc3_out` for 7.1B at L16384, resharding, swift SCA for 0.9B /
4B, CUDA-graph blocks; (3) the bandwidth contention between the FSDP weight-gradient GEMMs and the overlapped
reduce-scatter (440 vs 800 TFLOP/s on H100) should shrink. The plan lists the carry-over checks, what to re-tune
and what not to repeat.
